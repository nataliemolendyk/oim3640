## My Project Proposal

**What I'm building:** 
I am building a tip and bill splitter that calculates tip with the amount of the bill and the percentage of tip given at a restaurant. This will first calculate tax (based on which state the restaurant is in) and then add the tip onto the amount (tip amount is chosen by the user). The tax is be coded in a small table of rates.

**Why I chose this:** 
I chose this because I am struggling to figure out tips percentages in my head and I am not good at splitting the bill amount with an odd number of people (which usually happens a lot). I also find myself and my friends ordering meals that are different prices, yet we split the bill evenly, making it unfair for one or multiple people.

**Core features:** 
This calculator will take the inputed percentage of the tip and apply it to the bill amount.
It will convert the percentage to an amount.
It will sum the tip, tax and the amount together.
This calculator will then divide the bill evenly or based on the weighting of each person's amount (percentage of the bill each person's meal cost).
The user will input the amount of their bill into the calculator.
