# Ideas

## Week 4
A pain point I have is managing all of my different daily tasks and not getting burntout from not having free time. I have an app where I track my weekly schedule, but it is making me more stressed when it piles up. 

## Week 5
Something I can do to solve this pain point could be creating an app that estimates the time each activity takes and provides suggestions on which day of the week to do it on based on my schedule. This app could help with stress levels and managing burnout by delegating activities to certain parts of the day.

## Week 6
An issue I have with this app is some days are busier than others and I can't move the tasks to a different day. I do think that planning the amount of time each activity takes is important because it would help with planning out each day. Something that could be implemented in this app is a reminder to take breaks because even though I may think just pushing through is the best, at times, taking breaks and resetting helps energize myself.

## Week 7
A new pain point that I have come across is creating outfits each day without repeating. More specifically, I wanted to try out new outfits at the beginning of the year and I've noticed that I rewear the same pieces of clothing every week. I think an app or website could be beneficial for this pain point that tracks all of the clothes I have and I can create outfits without having to try everything on. I think merging this with Pinterest could help provide inspiration for my outfits with the clothes I have.

## Week 8
I think an app that randomly chooses different pieces of clothing from my closet and puts them together could solve this problem. I feel like this would get me out of my comfort zone and try new colors together. In order to make these outfits, the app would have to categorize the piece of clothing by type and then choose from each category. A feature that could be added on is a weather setting that takes the weather outside and bases the outfit off of that. So for example, if it was warm, the app wouldn't give me a sweater, it would know to give me a tshirt.
